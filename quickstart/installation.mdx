## Requirements

- **Anki 2.1.20+** with **Qt6** (all recent Anki versions ship with Qt6)
- A free Smart Notes account (you'll create one in step 2)

## Install the addon

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Anki and go to the Add-ons menu">
    In Anki, click **Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons...**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Smart Notes addon code">
    Enter the code below and click **OK**.

    ```
    1531888719
    ```

    Anki will download Smart Notes. Restart Anki when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    After restarting, open **Tools → Smart Notes**. You'll be prompted to sign in.

    Click **Sign in with browser**, complete the flow in your browser, and Smart Notes will auto-authenticate back into Anki.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You're ready to go. New accounts get a free trial — no credit card required.
</Check>
