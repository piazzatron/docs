Smart Notes is an Anki plugin that brings AI-powered card generation natively into Anki. Generate **text**, **audio**, and **images** for any field on your cards — one card at a time, or thousands at once.

It's designed to be intuitive yet powerful, so you don't need to juggle multiple Anki addons together.
