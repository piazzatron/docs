Smart Notes is an Anki plugin that brings AI-powered card generation natively into Anki. Generate **text**, **audio**, and **images** for any field on your cards — one card at a time, or thousands at once.

It's designed to be intuitive yet powerful, so you don't need to juggle multiple Anki addons together.

## What can it do?

- **Smart Fields** — define a prompt once, and AI fills in that field for every card automatically
- **Text, TTS, and Images** — generate any combination of content for a card
- **Bulk generation** — generate fields for thousands of cards with one click
- **Chained fields** — one Smart Field can feed into another (e.g. example sentence → image based on that sentence)
