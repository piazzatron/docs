Smart Notes currently includes these image generation models:

| Model | Relative cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Z-Image Turbo](https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo) | 0.3x | Fastest and cheapest, but lowest quality. |
| ⭐ [GPT Image 1.5 Low](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models/gpt-image-1.5) | 1x | Best pick right now. Usually the best balance of quality and price. |
| [Flux Dev](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-dev) | 3x | Fast, but may have quality issues. |
| [GPT Image 1.5](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models/gpt-image-1.5) | 4x | Higher-cost GPT Image 1.5 option. |
| [Nano Banana 2](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/nano-banana-2/) | 5x | Highest-cost image option. |
