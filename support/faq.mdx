## How do I get help or report a bug?

The fastest way to get help is the [Smart Notes Discord](https://discord.gg/kxGaWpkTGr).

You can also email [support@smart-notes.xyz](mailto:support@smart-notes.xyz) or report bugs on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/piazzatron/anki-smart-notes/issues).

## Does Smart Notes overwrite existing fields?

Smart Notes is designed to avoid overwriting fields you already filled in unless you explicitly choose to regenerate them.

## Can I generate cards in bulk?

Yes. Use the Anki browser to select notes, then right-click and choose **Generate Smart Fields**. You can also generate from a deck.

## Can one Smart Field use another Smart Field?

Yes. This is called chaining. See [Chained fields](/basics/chained-fields).
